Delete Your Kreashiv Account

If you wish to permanently delete your Kreashiv account, you can do so directly from the app.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the Kreashiv app.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion.
  5. Your account and associated data will be permanently removed.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Your account information.
  • Your profile information.
  • Your prompts and generated content stored in our database.
  • Your subscription information associated with the account.

Data That May Be Retained

Some information may be retained temporarily if required for legal obligations, fraud prevention, security, or financial record keeping.
