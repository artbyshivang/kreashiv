Kreashiv Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

We may collect your name, email address, profile information, prompt history, subscription details and app usage data to provide and improve Kreashiv services.

Account Information

Your account information is securely stored using trusted cloud services and is only used for authentication and personalization.

Prompt & Content Storage

Prompts and generated content may be stored to provide history, library features and improve the overall user experience.

Subscriptions & Payments

Premium purchases are processed securely through Google Play Billing. We do not store your payment card information.

Security

We use industry-standard security practices to protect your data.

Account Deletion

Users can permanently delete their account from the Settings page. When requested, personal data associated with the account will also be removed according to applicable laws.
